## 建議閱讀
_Claude 根據 wiki 缺口建議的來源。_

- Cai et al. (2010), *Microinsurance, Trust and Economic Development*, NBER WP 15396：隨機田野實驗，可以和本文的非實驗設計對照。
- Mosley (2003), *Micro-Insurance: Scope, Design and Assessment of Wider Impacts*：BRAC 的 MHI。
- Gertler & Gruber (2002), *Insuring Consumption Against Illness*, AER：非正式保險的保障程度。
- Pitt & Khandker (1998), JPE：孟加拉微型貸款效果的經典研究。
- *The role of micro health insurance in providing financial risk protection in developing countries — a systematic review*, BMC Public Health (2016)：[PDF](https://bmcpublichealth.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1186/s12889-016-2937-9)，跨國綜述。
- Churchill (ed.) (2006), *Protecting the Poor: A Microinsurance Compendium*, ILO：[聯合國數位圖書館](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/593737)，微型保險的定義與設計原則。
- 下載連結：Cai et al. 2010 [PDF](https://nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15396/w15396.pdf)、Gertler & Gruber [PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w8148/w8148.pdf)。
